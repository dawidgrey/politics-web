How to read this comparison

Published position means a specific proposal was found in attributable primary material. Position is ambiguous means primary material exists but does not support one clear reading. No clear position found means a targeted search of the listed official sources found no specific current proposal; it does not mean support, opposition or that the party has never spoken about the topic.

Summaries paraphrase party material neutrally. Open “Evidence” for the exact source, date, research note and pinpoint used. Policies and parliamentary representation can change after this research snapshot.
