Cost of living

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes abolishing net zero policy and using lower energy costs to reduce household expenses. It also proposes a $400 million food-security fund and tax deductions for farmers and producers who donate food.

Housing affordability

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes a $1 trillion home-ownership program, faster development approvals, satellite cities linked by fast rail and government land acquisition for housing. It also proposes a Housing Lending Authority offering loans priced two percentage points above government borrowing rates.

Health and Medicare

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes doubling the health budget, increasing hospital beds and offering incentives for health workers to serve regional areas. It says GP visits and other medical services should be free, funded from savings after abolishing the NDIS.

Energy

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes allowing private suppliers to compete across all forms of energy and using more Australian gas for domestic electricity generation. It proposes a 25% tax on exported gas and no tax on gas used for Australian power generation.

Taxation

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes a 25% tax on exported gas, a 25% export licence charge on iron ore and a 20% tax concession for people living more than 200 kilometres from a capital city. It says iron-ore receipts would be reserved for repaying national debt.

Jobs and the economy

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes balancing the federal budget, directing more superannuation investment into Australia and reducing restrictions on bank lending to resources projects. It also proposes reviewing the public service to remove duplicated work across levels of government.

Immigration

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes zero general immigration until infrastructure can absorb more arrivals. It would limit arrivals to family reunions and work permits for projects it considers nationally significant, and cap foreign students at 50,000 a year.

Education

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes fully government-funded universities, free merit-based education and cancellation of student debt. It also proposes limiting foreign students to 50,000 a year and directing Commonwealth funding to remove political and special-interest influence from classrooms.

Disability and the NDIS

Published position

The United Australia Party proposes abolishing the NDIS and replacing it with an Australian Care Benefit set 30% above current pension levels, with extra allowances for specific needs. It says former NDIS workers could move to other industries and that savings would fund health services.
