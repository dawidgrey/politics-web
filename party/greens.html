Cost of living

Published position

The Greens propose measures including action on supermarket price gouging, 50-cent public transport, free school lunches and wiping student debt. They say these measures would be funded by higher taxes on billionaires and large corporations.

Housing affordability

Published position

The Greens propose limiting rent increases, restricting property-investor tax concessions to one investment property and building more public housing. They also propose a regulated lower-cost mortgage product and stronger oversight of bank mortgage pricing.

Health and Medicare

Published position

The Greens propose adding dental care, unlimited mental-health care and ADHD assessments to Medicare, while funding public health services so patients can see a GP without a fee. Their platform presents these measures as part of universal access to health care.

Immigration

Published position

The Greens propose increasing the humanitarian intake to 50,000 places a year, adding an uncapped private sponsorship route and reducing family-reunion delays and costs. They also propose ending offshore detention, capping initial onshore detention at seven days and repealing several migration laws.

Education

Published position

The Greens propose free public education, including childcare, school, TAFE and university, and wiping student debt. They also propose free school lunches and an annual back-to-school payment.

Defence and national security

Published position

The Greens oppose AUKUS and advocate a foreign and defence policy centred on peace, human rights and nuclear disarmament. Their platform calls for ending Australia’s participation in AUKUS.

Disability and the NDIS

Published position

The Greens oppose cuts to the NDIS and say it should have enough funding and staff to support participants. They also propose accessible housing and higher Disability Support Pension and Carer Payment rates.

Published position

The Greens propose lower disclosure thresholds, caps on political donations and truth-in-political-advertising laws. They also propose restrictions linking political donations to eligibility for government contracts and grants.
