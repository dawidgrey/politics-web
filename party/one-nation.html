Cost of living

Published position

One Nation proposes cutting electricity bills through changes to the National Electricity Market, halving fuel excise and raising the tax-free threshold. It also supports income splitting for some families and higher Medicare rebates.

Housing affordability

Published position

One Nation proposes a five-year GST exemption for building materials used in new homes valued up to $1 million. It also calls for reviewing government fees, changing disability-access rules and stopping residential property sales to non-residents and non-citizens.

Health and Medicare

Published position

One Nation proposes three-year regional contracts for newly qualified health professionals, with their HECS-HELP debt paid in return. It also supports higher Medicare rebates and lower medicinal-cannabis access costs.

Energy

Published position

One Nation supports coal, nuclear and hydro generation and opposes the current net-zero framework. It also proposes changes to the National Electricity Market intended to cut bills by 20 per cent.

Taxation

Published position

One Nation proposes family income splitting, a higher tax-free threshold and a temporary GST exemption for some new-home building materials. It opposes a higher GST and death duties and says multinational companies should pay their fair share.

Jobs and the economy

Published position

One Nation proposes a larger national apprenticeship scheme and government investment in water and infrastructure. It also proposes spending reductions and a public oil-and-gas investment vehicle with a royalty replacing the petroleum resource rent tax.

Immigration

Published position

One Nation says immigration is too high and links it to pressure on housing, infrastructure, services and wages. The issues page states the concern but does not give a numerical migration target.

Disability and the NDIS

Published position

One Nation proposes reforming the NDIS as part of a wider spending-reduction plan. A separate current statement says it supports the scheme but wants means testing, registered and audited providers, tighter eligible supports and stronger penalties.

Nature and environment

Published position

One Nation proposes new dams, disclosure of water ownership and a ban on water sales to foreign investors. It also supports expanding forestry plantations and measures it says would sustain fisheries.

Government integrity

Published position

One Nation proposes citizen-initiated referendums so voters can put legislation or referendum questions forward. It also supports constitutionally protected free speech; these are governance proposals rather than an anti-corruption plan.
