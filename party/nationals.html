Cost of living

Published position

The Nationals proposed halving fuel excise for 12 months, which they said would reduce petrol prices by 25 cents a litre. The 2025 plan estimated a two-car household would save about $1,500 over the year.

Housing affordability

Published position

The Nationals proposed allowing eligible first-home buyers of new homes to deduct interest on the first $650,000 of their mortgage for five years. The wider plan also included expanded home guarantees, up to $50,000 of superannuation for a deposit and $5 billion for housing infrastructure.

Published position
