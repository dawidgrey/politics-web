Cost of living

Published position

The Liberal Party proposes lower income taxes, lower energy costs and lower immigration as its main response to falling living standards. Its plan includes indexing income-tax thresholds to inflation and pursuing more domestic energy supply.

Housing affordability

Published position

The Liberal Party proposes linking the annual net overseas migration ceiling to the number of new homes completed. It also proposes a $5 billion housing infrastructure fund and changes to the National Construction Code intended to lower building costs.

Evidence

Climate change

Published position

The Liberal Party says it would scrap net zero and repeal policies it describes as carbon taxes, including the safeguard mechanism, vehicle emissions standard and Capacity Investment Scheme. It would allow coal, gas, oil, nuclear, hydro, batteries and some renewables in the energy mix.

Energy

Published position

The Liberal Party proposes increasing Australian gas and oil production and allowing coal, gas, oil, nuclear, hydro, batteries and renewables where it considers them affordable. It says this approach is intended to lower power bills.

Taxation

Published position

The Liberal Party proposes indexing income-tax thresholds to inflation, a $50,000 instant asset write-off for small businesses and repeal of several Labor taxes. It also proposes putting 80 per cent of unexpected commodity revenue into a Future Generations Fund.

Jobs and the economy

Published position

The Liberal Party proposes lower taxes and regulation, reduced government spending and new legal duties for regulators to consider competition, investment, productivity, wages and jobs. It also proposes reserving most unexpected commodity revenue for debt reduction and infrastructure.

Immigration

Published position

The Liberal Party proposes capping immigration below the number of homes built. It says this would reduce the housing shortage within three years.
