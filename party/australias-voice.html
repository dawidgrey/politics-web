Cost of living

Published position

Australia’s Voice proposes laws allowing divestiture of major supermarkets that exploit consumers, raise prices or misuse market power. It also supports raising JobSeeker and indexing the Remote Area Allowance.

Housing affordability

Published position

Australia’s Voice proposes limiting negative gearing and reducing the capital gains tax discount from 50% to 25% over five years. It says the resulting revenue would support social housing and that the changes are intended to reduce rents and improve access to home ownership.

Government integrity

Published position

Australia’s Voice proposes freezing MPs’ pay rises and reducing political perks, including travel and vehicle allowances and payments to departing MPs. It also supports a public bank and a mandatory banking code, but does not publish a broader anti-corruption policy on this page.
